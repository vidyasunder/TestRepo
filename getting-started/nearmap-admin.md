# Nearmap Admin

MyAccount is the administration portal where you manage your organization's Nearmap subscription, users, and integrations. To get in, [log in to MyAccount](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/1663-log-in-to-myaccount) using your Nearmap credentials and familiarize yourself with the [MyAccount interface](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/646-myaccount-interface), which gives you a centralized view of your account. The [Administrator Dashboard](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/657-administrator-dashboard) is your starting point for day-to-day account management, surfacing key information about users, usage, and subscriptions at a glance. If your organization uses Single Sign-On, review the [Nearmap SSO](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/154-nearmap-single-sign-on-sso) documentation to understand how authentication is managed before you begin setting up users. For a full overview of what administrators can do in MyAccount, see [About MyAccount](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/122-about-myaccount).

### Invite users and manage permissions

As a Nearmap administrator, you are responsible for inviting team members and controlling what they can access. The invitation process differs depending on your subscription model - follow the [Current](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/1788-current-invite-users) guide if you are on the new subscription model (from August 2025), or the [Classic](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/658-classic-invite-users) guide if you are on the existing model. Once users are in your account, you can [manage user permissions](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/660-manage-permissions) to control access to products such as Advanced Export and GeoData Link, which require explicit permission grants and are not enabled by default. You can also [manage administrator privileges](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/663-manage-administrator-privileges) to promote users with admin access as your team structure evolves. If your organization uses workspaces to segment access across teams or departments, see [MyAccount Workspaces](https://help.nearmap.com/kb/articles/1666-myaccount-workspaces) for guidance on setting those up.
